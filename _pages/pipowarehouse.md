* Enables development and benchmarking of RL agents and combinatorial optimization algorithms
* Models real-world constraints: space, stacking, routing, and multi-agent coordination
* Foundation for AI-driven decision layers in next-generation WMS systems

**Tech:** Python, Reinforcement Learning, Operations Research, Simulation Systems
